What this prototype demonstrates

One problem. Solved completely.

Smart Buffer Enforcement

Buffer slots are automatically blocked after every meeting. Customers physically cannot book back-to-back — the slot simply doesn't appear.

Manager Team View

Sarah (manager) sees all 5 team members' schedules in one dashboard. Books on their behalf. Every booking reflects live across the whole team.

Live Across Both Views

Book a slot in the manager dashboard → open the customer booking page → that slot is already blocked with buffer. Real-time, no refresh needed.

Configurable Per Meeting Type

15-min quick sync gets 10 min buffer. 60-min deep dive gets 15 min. Each meeting type has its own recovery time — set by the manager.
